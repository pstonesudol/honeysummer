# Bouquet proposals (Phase 8)

**Implementation/local closeout complete — October 10, 2026.** See [`PHASE8_CLOSEOUT.md`](PHASE8_CLOSEOUT.md) for scope and verification evidence. Actual hosted Stripe/provider verification remains open in Phase 12.

Open a **bouquet** inquiry in `/admin/inquiries`, then choose **Start bouquet proposal**. Draft a customer-visible title, description, terms, itemized lines, fulfillment details and delivery charge. Save to see the exact preview; internal notes are not included in the invoice. Catalogue-backed lines take the numeric `FL-` listing ID; custom labor/flowers can be quoted without creating fake stock. Quotes never reserve stock.

**Send Stripe invoice** requires `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` even in local development. Stripe emails the invoice and hosts its secure payment page; do not send a second payment request. The invoice is due seven days after sending. Resend repeats Stripe's invoice email. Revise first voids an *open/unpaid* Stripe invoice, preserves its sent snapshot and opens a new draft. A paid invoice cannot be revised or voided. If sending/voiding fails ambiguously, inspect the Stripe Dashboard before doing anything else; never send a second invoice for the same payment by hand.

Configure the **same signed `/api/stripe/webhook/` endpoint** to receive `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, and `invoice.voided` in addition to the existing Checkout events. Run `python -m app.reconcile` to inspect missing payments, overdue/void invoices and unsent order confirmations; `--apply` retries safe transitions. Production scheduler activation and deployed provider/webhook acceptance belong to **Phase 12**, tracked in [`PRODUCTION_READINESS.md`](PRODUCTION_READINESS.md). Phase 8 retains local/test-mode functional verification; no live proposal payment is authorized until Phase 12 accepts the deployed invoice flow.

When an invoice is paid, the app creates a paid order once and journals any catalogue-backed quantities under row locks. Custom service lines are shown on the linked order but do not deduct inventory. If quoted catalogue stock has disappeared, the invoice remains **paid in Stripe**, but the proposal becomes `stock_review` without booking or stock deduction. The proposal page guides the owner through two paths:

- **Fulfill the original paid arrangement:** check physical stock and receive/replenish it through the inventory journal. Have the operator review `python -m app.reconcile` findings before running `--apply` (which can apply other safe recovery work too). Reconciliation now retries `stock_review` proposals and creates one order only after verified payment and sufficient stock. Do not fabricate stock, edit the sent snapshot or deduct a separate market sale.
- **Unable to fulfill:** agree the customer outcome, then use the proposal's verified invoice-payment refund controls, even when no order was booked. Import an existing Stripe refund rather than issuing another. Partial/uncertain refunds keep the proposal paused; any recorded refund blocks later automatic booking. Close the proposal only after its full refund is confirmed. Refunds never restock flowers or invent a paid order. Check for external/unrecorded refunds in Stripe before authorizing recovery.

Do **not** run the Checkout webhook for an invoice, charge the customer again, or silently substitute catalogue stock on a paid snapshot.
