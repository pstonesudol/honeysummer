# Bouquet proposals (Phase 8)

Open a **bouquet** inquiry in `/admin/inquiries`, then choose **Start bouquet proposal**. Draft a customer-visible title, description, terms, itemized lines, fulfillment details and delivery charge. Save to see the exact preview; internal notes are not included in the invoice. Catalogue-backed lines take the numeric `FL-` listing ID; custom labor/flowers can be quoted without creating fake stock. Quotes never reserve stock.

**Send Stripe invoice** requires `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` even in local development. Stripe emails the invoice and hosts its secure payment page; do not send a second payment request. The invoice is due seven days after sending. Resend repeats Stripe's invoice email. Revise first voids an *open/unpaid* Stripe invoice, preserves its sent snapshot and opens a new draft. A paid invoice cannot be revised or voided. If sending/voiding fails ambiguously, inspect the Stripe Dashboard before doing anything else; never send a second invoice for the same payment by hand.

Configure the **same signed `/api/stripe/webhook/` endpoint** to receive `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, and `invoice.voided` in addition to the existing Checkout events. Run `python -m app.reconcile` to inspect missing payments, overdue/void invoices and unsent order confirmations; `--apply` retries safe transitions. The reconciliation scheduler is a Phase 6/11 production gate. Test-mode invoice creation, payment and webhook delivery must be verified before taking a live proposal payment.

When an invoice is paid, the app creates a paid order once and journals any catalogue-backed quantities under row locks. Custom service lines are shown on the linked order but do not deduct inventory. If quoted catalogue stock has disappeared, the invoice remains **paid in Stripe**, but the proposal becomes `stock_review` without booking or stock deduction: replenish stock and run reconciliation with `--apply`, or resolve the customer/refund in Stripe and document the decision. Do **not** record a separate market sale or run the Checkout webhook for an invoice.

Current limitations before declaring Phase 8 launch-complete: no invoice-specific refund or cancellation admin control (handle in Stripe and reconcile manually), no automatic stock alternative/substitution workflow, no customer view tracking, no custom due-date or deposit policy, and no live Stripe invoice/payment verification yet. The admin only sends invoices for full upfront payment in USD, without automatic tax calculation. Confirm taxes, refunds and fulfillment policy with Isabella before production activation.
