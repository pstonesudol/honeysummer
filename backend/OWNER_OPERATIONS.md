# Owner operations (Phase 9, in progress)

Use **Invoice balances** in the admin navigation for a read-only list and CSV of active bouquet proposals and wedding quotes. It distinguishes the outstanding quote balance from the amount of invoices actually recorded as sent, and shows verified wedding partial payments and recorded invoice refunds separately. An unsent future installment is a quote balance, **not** an open invoice. Review/issuing rows may have an uncertain Stripe outcome; check Stripe before following up or sending again. Due dates are based on local agreed dates (or an estimated seven-day default), not live Stripe data. A refund does not automatically amend the agreed balance. This is not net revenue or a substitute for Stripe reconciliation; the separate **Sales ledger** tracks paid-order gross and recorded refunds.

**Products & waste** is a date-filtered report and CSV of product/order snapshots, channel/payment-method gross figures and explicitly journaled waste units. Fully refunded orders have separate original-gross rows, not negative net sales. A stock count discrepancy is not labeled waste, and the report does not value historical waste using current catalogue prices. **Needs attention** lists locally pending wedding/bouquet reviews, uncertain refunds and unsent order confirmations (including attempts); it does not call Stripe or replace the scheduled reconciliation job. The `app.reconcile` command now records its run findings (first 100 messages) there as well; a missing run may mean the job failed before recording. Check Stripe before retrying an ambiguous send.

**Site content** in the admin edits contact/location/service-area/pickup details, Instagram, six page introductions and up to eight FAQs. Blank values retain the current storefront wording; FAQs replace the defaults only when at least one complete pair is entered. Save, then check the live page (cached content may take up to five minutes) and the public `/api/site-content/` response. These are plain-text fields, not an HTML editor. Gallery images require alt text and can set focal X/Y percentages (0–100); a flower listing can reuse a gallery image instead of uploading another copy. The existing announcement editor accepts optional Eastern start/end calendar dates; active announcements appear only within their window. Apply migrations `a7b190e94cf1`, `c384f0c736c8` and `09cb3a8ee77a` before editing content.

**Persistent media:** set all of `R2_ENDPOINT_URL`, `R2_BUCKET`, `R2_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `R2_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`, and `R2_PUBLIC_URL` on the backend; the public URL must serve that bucket over HTTPS (configure a Cloudflare custom domain or public bucket URL separately). Image uploads use random immutable keys and accept only PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP up to 8 MB. Partial R2 configuration blocks uploads; do not deploy production relying on the local `/media` directory, which is ephemeral. Existing public gallery/listing media must be copied to R2 using its existing relative keys before switching the public URL. Customer inquiry uploads now use a **separate** `PRIVATE_MEDIA_ROOT` in development or `R2_PRIVATE_BUCKET` (without a public/CDN domain) in deployment. The private bucket must differ from `R2_BUCKET`, and the admin-only `/admin/inquiries/<id>/photo` route serves its bytes with no-store. A public R2 setup without a private bucket rejects new inquiry photos. **Previously uploaded inquiry photos remain in the old public /media tree and must be identified, moved to the private bucket/root, removed from public storage, and verified individually before claiming historical photos are private.** Do not accept sensitive images until that cleanup and access controls are verified. Never put R2 credentials in the frontend. Production verification of both buckets and retention is a Phase 11 gate.

The `/admin` dashboard shows paid, incomplete orders scheduled for today and the next seven days in **Eastern time**. Orders without a structured date still appear in the outstanding-order count but not the calendar list; add a date on the order detail page. The dashboard also shows unhandled inquiries, pending florist accounts, and up to 30 active listings at or below their configured low-stock threshold (default five units). The threshold is editable on each flower listing; zero alerts only when the listing is sold out.

**Booked weddings · payments outstanding** appears on both the dashboard (up to ten) and Orders page (up to 30). These are wedding quotes with at least one verified paid deposit/installment and a remaining balance; the rows link to the quote and show the event/customer, amount paid, balance, next payment/due date, and any review status. They are **not** paid orders or paid revenue and do not reserve catalogue inventory. An unpaid Stripe invoice remains on its bouquet proposal or wedding quote; a `pending` Order instead means a storefront Checkout with a temporary stock hold. If a wedding payment is only partially complete, plan the event from this booked-work section; the paid Order is created after the final verified payment. Quotes in `review` with a previous verified payment remain visible, but inspect Stripe before attempting another send.

Open a paid order to enter a date and optional time, set **New → Preparing → Ready → Completed**, and add private preparation notes. Completing it sets the existing fulfillment timestamp. Completed orders cannot be reopened from this screen. The activity trail records schedule/state changes and the operator; it intentionally does not repeat private notes. Use **Print packing slip** for item quantities, fulfillment/contact details, customer instructions, and private preparation notes. Treat printed slips as sensitive customer data. Bouquet invoice orders include the paid proposal's line-item snapshot. Scheduling and packing do not alter inventory or payment state.

Use each listing's Inventory page for audited restocks, waste, and market-sale adjustments; the dashboard alert is not a stock count. Continue to follow `INVENTORY_RUNBOOK.md` and `BOUQUET_PROPOSALS.md` for payments and stock.

## Additional owner tools

- **Record paid order** from Orders: enter catalogue listings or custom arrangements, customer details, cash or an externally collected payment reference, and the exact amount collected. The total is validated against current catalogue prices and listing-configured delivery fees; all catalogue deductions are journaled in one transaction. A repeated form submission cannot create a second order. Custom lines have no catalogue stock movement. Do not use this screen to request payment or to enter an unverified payment. Invoice proposals still use their separate Stripe flow. For Stripe Dashboard Tap to Pay, follow `IN_PERSON_SALES.md` and record the Dashboard reference in the order rather than additionally recording a stand-alone market-sale movement.
- **Bouquet/retail invoice proposals** from bouquet inquiries: start with one itemized line and use **Add line item / Remove line item** for up to 200 catalogue or custom lines. Save and reopen the draft without losing lines. Catalogue references are not reserved until the Stripe invoice is paid; the separate **Record paid order** form does not send an invoice.
- **Shop-wide stock** shows available, pending-order reserved and on-hand balances. Bulk harvest/restock lines are combined per listing and applied atomically with a shared source and reason. On each listing's Inventory page, a physical count reconciles *on hand* (including held stock) without releasing pending reservations. Counts below reserved stock are rejected. Both correction and no-change counts leave an audit entry.
- **Inventory movement history** on each listing can now be filtered by action or reason/source, paged through in groups of 100, and exported as CSV with the same filters. CSV includes all matching movements, not only the visible page. An exported adjustment's signed delta is not a new payment or sale.
- **Catalogue management** includes an inactive zero-stock duplicate of any listing and atomic bulk activation, channel, season or price updates by listing ID (up to 100 at once). Filter the listing page by season (spring, summer, fall, winter, year-round); season tags do not automatically hide listings from the storefront. Existing order price snapshots and inventory are never rewritten by these changes. Duplicate images currently reuse the same stored path; verify the source image still exists before publishing.
- **Customer history** looks up an exact email across guest/wholesale orders and inquiries. Inquiry detail has a stage, private notes, follow-up date and a manually entered sent/received correspondence log; due follow-ups appear on the dashboard. Recording correspondence does **not** send an email, import a mailbox or create an invoice. New website inquiry acknowledgement attempts are recorded automatically only when the provider accepts them; this is **not** proof of delivery. Farm and customer email submissions are attempted independently, and failures appear in Needs attention. Local console mail is labeled "logged locally." An existing mailbox conversation is still entered manually. Avoid logging sensitive payment information. Apply migration `d64be6148b53`.
- **Payment refunds** on an order: enter a reason and amount up to the remaining original payment. For cash/external orders, first return the funds off-site, then confirm and record the transaction reference; recording does **not** move money. For Stripe Checkout orders with a saved payment intent, the form initiates a Stripe refund against that intent with a stable idempotency key. A repeat submission cannot initiate a second refund, and an ambiguous/unfinished Stripe result blocks all further attempts pending manual Stripe review. Full refunds set the order's payment status to refunded; partial refunds retain paid status with a separate refund journal. **No refund restores stock**: restock only on confirmed physical return through the separate action. Bouquet/wedding Stripe *invoice* orders use their invoice detail page, **not** the Checkout refund form. Apply migration `751e841829ae` before using refund controls.

**Wedding invoice revision/recovery (local):** On a known sent invoice, **Void for revision** verifies the exact unpaid open invoice and amount in Stripe, then voids it with an idempotency key. The voided invoice remains in history; a replacement uses the next revision and fresh Stripe keys. If a void webhook arrived first, **Reopen verified void payment** rechecks the remote void. If Stripe's void result is uncertain, the quote and invoice stop in review; **Verify uncertain void outcome** checks if that invoice is still open or void. An invoice send with no saved ID can only be attached after finding its exact invoice in Stripe: the attachment verifies quote/step/revision/amount metadata and never sends another invoice. An exact *paid* invoice also requires Stripe to confirm full payment before it creates the booked/paid state; a void invoice stays void until separately reopened. An unmatched invoice must not be attached. **Do not retry quote #9 without confirming its actual Stripe outcome.** Migration `7e18b051c429` preserves all revisions. Later payment dates and post-refund agreements still need explicit review; no customer's plan changes automatically.

**Invoice-level Stripe refunds (local, test doubles only):** A verified paid wedding installment/deposit or bouquet proposal has an individual refund form. It checks a single paid Stripe InvoicePayment, matching PaymentIntent and original Charge, then requests an idempotent refund for no more than the invoice's unrefunded amount. The invoice refund journal (`c8a446f8a28d`) and, when a paid order exists, the order refund journal record the payment without returning stock. Every refund pauses the quote/proposal in **review**, so further wedding invoice sends cannot run automatically. Refunds with a saved Stripe refund ID can be refreshed from Stripe. An ambiguous result without an ID cannot safely be retried; reconcile external refunds before requesting another. After **every** paid invoice is completely refunded and no open/uncertain invoice remains, the owner can close the quote as cancelled (no stock movement). After a *partial* wedding refund, the owner can enter a **new itemized agreement** only when all older invoices are paid/void and refunds confirmed, with a written customer acceptance reference, checkbox and separate new due date. The retained verified payments (gross receipts less refunds) are credited; the amended amount due must be at least $0.50. Saving does not send anything. A manually confirmed revised-balance invoice is then the **only** new collection step, with its own Stripe invoice and immutable agreement snapshot. If its paid event agrees with retained payments and new balance, it creates one paid order based on the revised itemization. The old quote/invoices remain in history; no catalogue stock is reserved. Apply migration `83e31c55ab49`. The invoice balance report shows original receipts and recorded refunds separately; never treat a pre-revision quote balance as a newly authorized charge. This path has automated tests but has **not** been exercised against Stripe test-mode payments or owner-accepted.
- **Sales ledger** lists paid and fully refunded orders with payment provenance, original gross, recorded successful refunds and recorded net amounts. The Eastern-date filter and CSV export are available at `/admin/reports/sales`. Older fully refunded orders with no refund journal show **Unknown** rather than an invented zero. Refunds still in review, Stripe fees, taxes and unpaid invoices are excluded; do not use this as a tax or cash-basis accounting report.

**Still open in Phase 9:** real Stripe test-mode acceptance of invoice refund/revision and resolution of absent-ID ambiguous sends without a match; inbound/external email correspondence import (manual notes are available), broader cross-page media selection, live Stripe refund reconciliation, complete accessible branded admin polish and owner acceptance. Site copy and R2 upload infrastructure exist locally but need real assets and owner review. Production Stripe, R2 and Resend credentials and sign-off belong to Phase 11.

## Wedding/event quotes (test mode until verified)

From a **wedding inquiry**, start a quote, use **Add line item / Remove line item** to build the itemized floral/service offer (one row initially; up to 200 rows per quote), and choose **full upfront**, **deposit plus balance**, or **installments**. Unrelated fields are hidden and disabled in the admin: for example, Full upfront does not submit a stale deposit amount. For installments, start with two payments and use **Add payment / Remove payment** as needed (up to 200). Enter each as dollars or a percentage of the quote total, with strictly increasing due dates and at least $0.50 per payment. The final dollar amounts must add up to the full quote exactly; an all-percentage plan totaling 100% assigns rounding pennies to its last payment. The form shows a running total, but the server performs the authoritative integer-cent validation. Each invoice can be sent on owner confirmation or on its own scheduled date. A later invoice waits until the previous invoice is fully paid in Stripe. Save to preview all quote lines and payment amounts/dates. Sending or approving a schedule locks the quote's terms. The first verified deposit or installment books work but does **not** create a paid order or reserve today's seasonal inventory. Only the verified **final** payment creates the paid custom order and queues confirmation mail. Each invoice has its own immutable amount and ID; duplicates and mismatches are rejected. The quote screen can refresh a recorded invoice's paid/void state from Stripe if a webhook was missed.

At quote creation, set a due date for each invoice (Eastern calendar dates, ending at 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern in Stripe) and choose **send on confirmation** or **send automatically on date** for each applicable payment. Deposit/full-upfront quotes can leave a due date blank to use Stripe's seven-day default for manual sends; installment due dates are required. Saving a draft does not send anything: **Approve and schedule first invoice** freezes an automatically scheduled quote. A subsequent automatic invoice is not sent until both its selected date has arrived **and** Stripe has verified the preceding payment. If a payment arrives after the next send date, the job sends that next invoice only if its agreed due date has not passed; otherwise it reports a failure for manual review rather than silently moving the due date or sending an overdue invoice. For a manual installment, use **Send installment N invoice** after the previous payment is verified.

In local Docker, `wedding-scheduler` runs the independent job every minute. Outside Docker, run `uv run python -m app.wedding_schedule --apply` from `backend/` on an independent schedule (for example, every five minutes); omit `--apply` for a report-only dry run. Monitor nonzero exits and `needs review` findings. This job must have the same database and Stripe test/live environment as the API. **Railway production scheduling/alerting remains Phase 11 setup**; selecting automatic mode does not activate it in production. The job never sends unapproved drafts or duplicates a recorded invoice.

**Important:** Stripe's native installment payment plans are private preview; dated invoices and optional automatic sending here use a separate ordinary Stripe invoice for each payment and an independent app job, not that preview feature. The customer does not get a publicly hosted quote page; invoice lines include the scope, payment plan, notes and terms. There is no automatic catalogue allocation for future wedding florals. Refunds, invoice revision/void recovery, overdue reminders and net-balance reporting still need work. If an issue leaves a quote in `review`, inspect Stripe and reconcile manually; do not send another invoice. Before using this with real customers, complete refund/cancellation controls and production webhook setup.

**Stripe sandbox verification (October 8, 2026):** With a test-mode key and a separate invoice-only CLI listener forwarding to `/api/stripe/webhook/`, a $25 test quote took a $10 deposit and $15 balance via two real test-mode Stripe invoices. Each paid event received HTTP 200; the deposit booked work without an order, and the balance created one paid custom order with confirmation outbox entries sent through the local console mail fallback. A separate $2 full-upfront quote also created one paid order after a signed `invoice.paid` event. Synthetic `@example.com` addresses were used; no live charge or customer email was sent. These test records (quotes #1 and #2, orders #16 and #17) remain in local development data. The temporary invoice listener was stopped; the existing Checkout-only listener was left untouched. Add invoice events to the persistent webhook configuration before further invoice testing or production use.

Stripe returned an open $1 invoice due **October 15 at 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern**. The test invoice was then voided; the quote is `void`. **Multi-installment sandbox check:** A synthetic eight-line, $4 quote (#7) was paid in three separate Stripe test-mode invoices ($1 + $1 + $2). The first and second signed payment events left the quote `installment_paid` with no order. After the final signed event the quote became `paid`, one order (#18) was created with eight snapshotted items, and the confirmation outbox was sent.
