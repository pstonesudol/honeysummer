# Owner operations (Phase 9, first slice)

The `/admin` dashboard shows paid, incomplete orders scheduled for today and the next seven days in **Eastern time**. Orders without a structured date still appear in the outstanding-order count but not the calendar list; add a date on the order detail page. The dashboard also shows unhandled inquiries, pending florist accounts, and up to 30 active listings at or below their configured low-stock threshold (default five units). The threshold is editable on each flower listing; zero alerts only when the listing is sold out.

Open a paid order to enter a date and optional time, set **New → Preparing → Ready → Completed**, and add private preparation notes. Completing it sets the existing fulfillment timestamp. Completed orders cannot be reopened from this screen. The activity trail records schedule/state changes and the operator; it intentionally does not repeat private notes. Use **Print packing slip** for item quantities, fulfillment/contact details, customer instructions, and private preparation notes. Treat printed slips as sensitive customer data. Bouquet invoice orders include the paid proposal's line-item snapshot. Scheduling and packing do not alter inventory or payment state.

Use each listing's Inventory page for audited restocks, waste, and market-sale adjustments; the dashboard alert is not a stock count. Continue to follow `INVENTORY_RUNBOOK.md` and `BOUQUET_PROPOSALS.md` for payments and stock.

## Additional owner tools

- **Record paid order** from Orders: enter catalogue listings or custom arrangements, customer details, cash or an externally collected payment reference, and the exact amount collected. The total is validated against current catalogue prices and listing-configured delivery fees; all catalogue deductions are journaled in one transaction. A repeated form submission cannot create a second order. Custom lines have no catalogue stock movement. Do not use this screen to request payment or to enter an unverified payment. Invoice proposals still use their separate Stripe flow. For Stripe Dashboard Tap to Pay, follow `IN_PERSON_SALES.md` and record the Dashboard reference in the order rather than additionally recording a stand-alone market-sale movement.
- **Bouquet/retail invoice proposals** from bouquet inquiries: start with one itemized line and use **Add line item / Remove line item** for up to 200 catalogue or custom lines. Save and reopen the draft without losing lines. Catalogue references are not reserved until the Stripe invoice is paid; the separate **Record paid order** form does not send an invoice.
- **Shop-wide stock** shows available, pending-order reserved and on-hand balances. Bulk harvest/restock lines are combined per listing and applied atomically with a shared source and reason. On each listing's Inventory page, a physical count reconciles *on hand* (including held stock) without releasing pending reservations. Counts below reserved stock are rejected. Both correction and no-change counts leave an audit entry.
- **Catalogue management** includes an inactive zero-stock duplicate of any listing and atomic bulk activation, channel or price updates by listing ID (up to 100 at once). Existing order price snapshots and inventory are never rewritten by these changes. Duplicate images currently reuse the same stored path; verify the source image still exists before publishing.
- **Customer history** looks up an exact email across guest/wholesale orders and inquiries. Inquiry detail has a stage, private notes and follow-up date; due follow-ups appear on the dashboard. This does not send correspondence or create wedding invoices.
- **Sales ledger** lists paid and fully refunded orders, with payment provenance and original gross totals. The Eastern-date filter and CSV export are available at `/admin/reports/sales`. Refunded rows show the *original total*, not net refunds; partial refunds, fees, taxes, unpaid invoices and balances are not represented. Do not use this as a tax or cash-basis accounting report.

**Still open in Phase 9:** production-safe wedding invoice revision/void/refund and acceptance testing, initiating refunds (especially partial), customer correspondence history, seasonal catalogue organization, media/R2 and storefront content, richer reporting, complete accessible branded admin polish, and owner acceptance. Production Stripe, R2 and Resend credentials and sign-off belong to Phase 11.

## Wedding/event quotes (test mode until verified)

From a **wedding inquiry**, start a quote, use **Add line item / Remove line item** to build the itemized floral/service offer (one row initially; up to 200 rows per quote), and choose **full upfront**, **deposit plus balance**, or **installments**. Unrelated fields are hidden and disabled in the admin: for example, Full upfront does not submit a stale deposit amount. For installments, start with two payments and use **Add payment / Remove payment** as needed (up to 200). Enter each as dollars or a percentage of the quote total, with strictly increasing due dates and at least $0.50 per payment. The final dollar amounts must add up to the full quote exactly; an all-percentage plan totaling 100% assigns rounding pennies to its last payment. The form shows a running total, but the server performs the authoritative integer-cent validation. Each invoice can be sent on owner confirmation or on its own scheduled date. A later invoice waits until the previous invoice is fully paid in Stripe. Save to preview all quote lines and payment amounts/dates. Sending or approving a schedule locks the quote's terms. The first verified deposit or installment books work but does **not** create a paid order or reserve today's seasonal inventory. Only the verified **final** payment creates the paid custom order and queues confirmation mail. Each invoice has its own immutable amount and ID; duplicates and mismatches are rejected. The quote screen can refresh a recorded invoice's paid/void state from Stripe if a webhook was missed.

At quote creation, set a due date for each invoice (Eastern calendar dates, ending at 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern in Stripe) and choose **send on confirmation** or **send automatically on date** for each applicable payment. Deposit/full-upfront quotes can leave a due date blank to use Stripe's seven-day default for manual sends; installment due dates are required. Saving a draft does not send anything: **Approve and schedule first invoice** freezes an automatically scheduled quote. A subsequent automatic invoice is not sent until both its selected date has arrived **and** Stripe has verified the preceding payment. If a payment arrives after the next send date, the job sends that next invoice only if its agreed due date has not passed; otherwise it reports a failure for manual review rather than silently moving the due date or sending an overdue invoice. For a manual installment, use **Send installment N invoice** after the previous payment is verified.

In local Docker, `wedding-scheduler` runs the independent job every minute. Outside Docker, run `uv run python -m app.wedding_schedule --apply` from `backend/` on an independent schedule (for example, every five minutes); omit `--apply` for a report-only dry run. Monitor nonzero exits and `needs review` findings. This job must have the same database and Stripe test/live environment as the API. **Railway production scheduling/alerting remains Phase 11 setup**; selecting automatic mode does not activate it in production. The job never sends unapproved drafts or duplicates a recorded invoice.

**Important:** Stripe's native installment payment plans are private preview; dated invoices and optional automatic sending here use a separate ordinary Stripe invoice for each payment and an independent app job, not that preview feature. The customer does not get a publicly hosted quote page; invoice lines include the scope, payment plan, notes and terms. There is no automatic catalogue allocation for future wedding florals. Refunds, invoice revision/void recovery, overdue reminders and net-balance reporting still need work. If an issue leaves a quote in `review`, inspect Stripe and reconcile manually; do not send another invoice. Before using this with real customers, complete refund/cancellation controls and production webhook setup.

**Stripe sandbox verification (October 8, 2026):** With a test-mode key and a separate invoice-only CLI listener forwarding to `/api/stripe/webhook/`, a $25 test quote took a $10 deposit and $15 balance via two real test-mode Stripe invoices. Each paid event received HTTP 200; the deposit booked work without an order, and the balance created one paid custom order with confirmation outbox entries sent through the local console mail fallback. A separate $2 full-upfront quote also created one paid order after a signed `invoice.paid` event. Synthetic `@example.com` addresses were used; no live charge or customer email was sent. These test records (quotes #1 and #2, orders #16 and #17) remain in local development data. The temporary invoice listener was stopped; the existing Checkout-only listener was left untouched. Add invoice events to the persistent webhook configuration before further invoice testing or production use.

**Dated scheduling check:** An approved test-mode quote #4 with automatic sending on October 8 and a chosen October 15 due date was picked up by `app.wedding_schedule --apply`. Stripe returned an open $1 invoice due **October 15 at 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern**. The test invoice was then voided; the quote is `void`. This tests sending and Stripe's due date, not production scheduling or overdue reminders. The local Docker `wedding-scheduler` service was started. Monitor it with `docker compose logs wedding-scheduler`; it does not configure a production scheduler. An invoice-only CLI listener subsequently appeared in another terminal; do not assume it will remain running after local development stops.

**Multi-installment sandbox check:** A synthetic eight-line, $4 quote (#7) was paid in three separate Stripe test-mode invoices ($1 + $1 + $2). The first and second signed payment events left the quote `installment_paid` with no order. After the final signed event the quote became `paid`, one order (#18) was created with eight snapshotted items, and the confirmation outbox was sent. Each hosted invoice included eight informational scope lines plus the amount payable for that installment. A first draft attempt (#6) hit Stripe's 500-character invoice memo limit; it was left in `review` without a charge. The code now caps the memo and moves the full quote breakdown, design notes, event details and terms into invoice line items. Do not retry #6 without manual Stripe review.
